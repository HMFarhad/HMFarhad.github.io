I believe good engineering is a series of small, considered decisions.

I show up, work through the problem, and keep improving the result. A working solution is the start; questioning it, simplifying it, and making it dependable is the other half of the job.

Today, I’m a Software Engineer at Nexetic Oy. My work connects backend systems, APIs, and thoughtful interfaces. When something is unfamiliar, I’m happy to be the person who dives in and learns.

I use AI as a learning partner, stay curious about the details, and care about building things that people can rely on.
